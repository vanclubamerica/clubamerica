About the Chapter

Student-run. Community-driven.

Club America at Van High School is a chapter of the national Club America organization, planned, led, and run entirely by Van High School students.

What We Are

A chapter of a national organization, run our own way

Club America is a student civic organization with chapters at high schools across the country. The Van High School chapter is affiliated with that national organization, which means we share its name and its broader mission, but this chapter operates independently. There is no adult-run agenda handed down to us; our officers set the calendar, choose the discussion topics, and plan the events.

That independence is what makes this chapter feel different from a typical school club. Members help build the meetings they attend.

What Makes Us Unique

Built by Van High School students, for Van High School students

Our meetings are short, focused, and student-led, just 30 minutes every Friday, which keeps discussion sharp and easy to fit into a busy school week. Officers rotate responsibility for planning topics, inviting guest speakers, and organizing community projects, so leadership experience isn't limited to one or two people at the top.

We keep the conversation open to different viewpoints. Civil, respectful discussion is a core part of how we operate.

Our Mission

To give Van High School students a genuine voice in civic life, through leadership experience, respectful discussion, and community involvement that starts in high school and lasts well beyond it.

Our Values

Respect for differing opinions, student ownership of every decision, and a commitment to learning that goes beyond the textbook, through real conversation and real projects.

What We Do

Weekly meetings, guest speakers, community service projects, current-events discussions, and student-led leadership initiatives throughout the school year.

A Look Inside

What a typical semester looks like

Meetings & Discussions

Every Friday, members gather for a focused 30-minute meeting built around a current event, historical topic, or piece of local government, always with room for open, respectful discussion.

Guest Speakers

Throughout the year we invite local officials, community leaders, and professionals to share perspective on government, economics, and civic life.

Community Projects

Members plan and carry out service projects that give back to Van and build practical experience in organizing and teamwork.

Leadership Development

Officer roles, committee leads, and event planning give members a chance to practice real leadership, not just hold a title.

Ready to get involved?

Membership is open to any Van High School student. It only takes a few minutes to let us know you're interested.
