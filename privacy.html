Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you visit or interact with this website.

Privacy Last updated: September 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Club America at Van High School ("Club America," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and handled when you visit this website or interact with forms, resources, and other services made available through it.

By using this website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We May Collect

The information collected depends on how you interact with the website. We may receive information that you voluntarily submit, including information provided through contact, membership, interest, event, or other forms.

This may include information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Contact information
  • School or organization information
  • Information included in a message or form submission
  • Other information you voluntarily provide

You are not required to provide personal information simply to browse publicly available portions of this website.

3. Automatically Collected Information

Like many websites, this site may receive limited technical information when you visit. Depending on the services used by the website, this may include information such as your browser type, device type, approximate location, referring page, or general website usage information.

This information may be provided by website hosting, analytics, security, form, or other third-party services used to operate the site.

4. How We Use Information

Information submitted through this website may be used for legitimate Club America activities, including:

  • Responding to questions and messages
  • Communicating with students, families, and community members
  • Managing club membership or interest
  • Organizing meetings and events
  • Maintaining club records
  • Operating and improving the website
  • Protecting the website from misuse or abuse

We do not use submitted information for purposes unrelated to the operation and activities of the club unless otherwise disclosed or permitted by applicable law.

5. Forms and Third-Party Services

Some forms, calendars, embedded resources, or other website functions may be provided by third-party services. When you interact with one of these services, information you provide may be processed according to that provider's own privacy policy and terms.

Club America does not control the privacy practices of third-party services and encourages users to review the applicable policies of those services before submitting information through them.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use cookies or similar technologies when required by the services used to operate the website.

Cookies may be used for functions such as maintaining website functionality, remembering preferences, security, analytics, or understanding general website usage.

You can generally control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the operation of some website features.

7. Information Sharing

Club America does not sell personal information submitted through this website.

Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to operate the website, provide a requested service, administer club activities, comply with legal obligations, protect the rights or safety of individuals, or prevent fraud, abuse, or security issues.

8. Student and Minor Privacy

Club America at Van High School may involve students who are under 18 years of age. We encourage students to avoid submitting unnecessary personal or sensitive information through public website forms.

Information concerning students should be handled only for legitimate club, school, organizational, or communication purposes and in accordance with applicable school policies and law.

9. Publicly Displayed Information

Certain information may be intentionally displayed publicly through the website, such as officer names, positions, club announcements, event information, or sponsor information.

Personal information should not be submitted for public display unless the individual or an appropriate authorized party has provided permission where required.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through the website. However, no website, online service, or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should avoid submitting passwords, financial account information, Social Security numbers, medical information, or other highly sensitive information through general website forms unless specifically requested through an appropriate secure process.

11. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, maintain appropriate organizational records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or maintain the security and operation of the website.

12. Children's Privacy

This website is not intended to independently collect extensive personal information from children. Where information from students or minors is collected in connection with club activities, it should be limited to information reasonably necessary for the relevant activity or communication.

13. External Links

This website may contain links to websites or services operated by third parties. Club America is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, content, or policies of external websites.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external website before submitting personal information.

14. Your Choices

Depending on the circumstances, you may request information about personal information you have submitted through this website or ask that inaccurate information be corrected.

Requests should include enough information for us to understand what information or submission the request concerns.

15. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the website, club operations, or applicable requirements change.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the beginning of this policy will be updated accordingly.

16. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled through this website, please contact Club America at Van High School through the Contact page.
