Who We Are

A student-run chapter of the national Club America organization

Club America at Van High School gives students a place to lead, learn, and get involved close to home. We're a chapter of the national Club America organization, but every meeting, event, and decision here is planned and run by Van High School students.

Members explore government, economics, history, and current events through honest discussion, guest speakers, and hands-on projects while building the kind of leadership and communication skills that carry well beyond high school.

Our Purpose

To develop confident, informed student leaders who understand how government and community work and who are ready to contribute to both.

Why Join

What membership gives you

Club America is built around four things every member gets, from their very first meeting.

Leadership

Run meetings, lead projects, and take on officer roles that build real, practical leadership experience.

Community

Take part in service projects and events that make a visible difference at Van High School and beyond.

Networking

Meet fellow students, teachers, guest speakers, and local sponsors who share an interest in civic life.

Learning

Learn about government, economics, history, and current events through free discussion.
